## v24 — Handoff Documentation Package for Parent-App Integration (10 Nov 2026)

**No application changes this round.** The prototype (`logitrack_prototype.html`) is byte-identical to the v23x build. This round produced a documentation package only, at the owner's request, so that a *different* Claude account — with no access to this conversation history — can rebuild or integrate LogiTrack end to end with no further instruction.

**Context.** The owner's plan is to integrate LogiTrack into a separate parent application he already runs. He asked for "as detailed as possible requirement and functional doc so that another Claude account can understand the application end to end and without any further instruction, it can create or replicate this application in that as is or better only."

**Scoping decisions taken with the owner (via a four-question clarification):**

1. **Technology-agnostic.** The parent application's stack was not yet decided, so the spec states *what* to build and leaves *how* (framework, database, platform) to the receiving team. The only architectural constraints stated are the non-functional ones that genuinely cannot be deferred — chiefly offline-first.
2. **As-built, with improvements clearly walled off.** Sections 2–12 of the functional spec document the system exactly as it exists and are marked authoritative. Section 13 holds every recommendation and known gap, explicitly flagged as not part of the current system.
3. **Deliverables:** a Markdown spec, a copy of the prototype as a runnable reference, a short covering brief to paste into the receiving account — and, added by the owner on top of the offered options, **a full BRD with detailed business flow and scenarios.**

**Documents produced (three new files):**

- **`LogiTrack_BRD.md`** (~531 lines) — Business Requirements Document. Business context and economics of the brokerage; the two parallel money relationships (party side at booking rate, truck side at hire rate) and why conflating them is the core source of error; stakeholders; seven quantified pain points; objectives with success measures; scope in/out/deferred with the reasoning for each exclusion; **a 40-term domain glossary** (bilty, freight challan, To-Pay, munshiyana, kanta, dala, shortage cutting, etc.) marked as required reading; the end-to-end lifecycle with both paths diagrammed and a table of exactly where they diverge; **nine worked numeric scenarios**; a catalogue of **53 numbered, individually testable business rules**; reporting needs; risks with mitigations; phasing; and acceptance conditions.
- **`LogiTrack_Functional_Spec.md`** (~1,436 lines) — the build specification. Reading order; system overview including the "computed figures are never stored" architectural rule; all reference data and enumerations; the complete data model (every entity, every field, type, required-ness, normalisation and notes, plus all embedded sub-record shapes and an entity-relationship diagram); navigation and the full 39-screen inventory; nine cross-cutting behaviours specified once (autocomplete, quick-add-with-return, silent masters, selective sharing, no native dialogs, etc.); module-by-module functional specs; **the complete calculation engine as annotated pseudocode, both branches, with every business rule cross-referenced inline**; the derived presentation-direction table; cross-module automation; all three output formats; design tokens and component vocabulary; 13 non-functional requirements; a seven-part acceptance-criteria set; and the separated recommendations section.
- **`LogiTrack_Handoff_Brief.md`** — a one-page covering note the owner pastes into the receiving account's first message, plus private notes for the owner himself.

**Verification — the scenarios were executed, not hand-worked.** `computeFreightChallanCalc` was extracted from the prototype into a standalone Node module (syntax-checked) and driven with nine constructed inputs, so every rupee figure in the BRD and in the spec's acceptance table is the engine's actual output. Verified outputs: **A** (To-Pay, consignee, full collection → recover ₹3,700); **A0** (same pre-POD, as estimate); **B** (partial collection ₹15,000 → the direction *reverses* to ₹2,000 extra payable to the driver, with ₹5,700 still due from the party); **B2** (B closed out → settled); **C** (consignor pays, 0.5 MT shortage with 0.2 MT tolerance and ₹500/MT cutting → ₹650 deduction, recover ₹4,075); **C2** (same shortage but consignee pays → shortage exemption engages, halting passes through to the party, recover ₹4,000); **D** (To-Be-Billed with halting, 1% TDS, ₹10,000 advance, ₹5,000 paid → balance ₹5,508); **E** (bilty-rate override ₹1,250/MT → party side moves, truck side unchanged, recover ₹5,500); **F** (fixed rates both sides → shortage recorded but deducts nothing, recover ₹5,000).

**Cross-check pass against the source, which caught four documentation errors before delivery** (all in the draft spec, none in the app):

1. Product Master's UOM list was written as `Bag/MT/Box/Nos/Ltr/Qtl/Pcs`; it is actually `MT/KG/Box/Bag/Piece`. The 22-value `COMMON_UNITS` list is a *separate* free-text suggestion datalist on the Bilty's goods-line Unit field — the two had been conflated, and are now documented as distinct, with a note on why both exist.
2. The freight-challan Advance "Paid To" options were written as five values; there are only two (`Driver`, `Owner`).
3. The Bilty's own Expenses Type picker was written as a short bespoke list (Kanta/Brokerage/Halting/Other); it actually reuses the **full Charge Type Master list**, with those names appearing only as examples in the field hint.
4. The screen count was stated as 38; programmatically counting the `render()` dispatcher map gives **39**.

Also verified directly against source and found correct as documented: date-grouping on exactly the three transaction lists; account switching living on the Company Profile detail screen; the driver suggestion row showing `name · mobile · DL` and the truck row `number · type`; the WhatsApp mobile-normalisation algorithm (now documented step-exact rather than paraphrased); the 8-suggestion cap; and that the Consignor/Consignee pickers search the **unfiltered** party list — note that the code comment above `attachBiltyAutocompletes` still claims they are filtered by Party Type, which is **stale** (the filter was removed in Round 23n). The code is right; the comment is not.

**Two pre-existing inconsistencies surfaced by the documentation work, and recorded rather than silently fixed:**

- **Payment Management scope mismatch** (spec §13.6). The module's stated intent — including its own in-source comment — is to show each Freight Challan's ledger entries *alongside* standalone entries in one combined view. `renderPaymentList` only ever reads `paymentEntries`, so the combined view was never built. Flagged for the owner to decide the intended scope before it is inherited by another team.
- **Percentage Commission freezes, percentage TDS does not** (spec §13.5). A percentage commission computes its amount at entry time and stores the result, so a later correction to weight or hire rate leaves it stale; TDS recomputes live on every read. Recommended aligning them, with the caveat that a stored commission amount may have been deliberately rounded or renegotiated — so it needs the owner's confirmation, not a unilateral change.

**Also carried into the spec's recommendations section:** the Owner-is-also-a-Driver linking design from the previous round (optional `ownerDriverId` on the truck, autocomplete against Driver Master, both reverse views derived rather than stored) — still awaiting the owner's go-ahead and his answers on three open questions; the party-side receivables ledger as the single largest gap and the most natural integration point with the parent application; real persistence, authentication and a change-audit log; real document storage, since every photo upload and PDF export in the prototype is a stub; and the small consistency clean-ups (legacy `bilty.advance` and `freightChallan.commStatus` fields, loose `==` id comparison, money stored as strings).

**Status after this round:** PRD Status line advanced to v0.24.0. Prototype unchanged at the v23x build. No regression suite was re-run, as no application code was touched.

### Bugs found & fixed during verification (before delivery)

1. **FAB "+" button crash on Bilty:** Button handlers were assigned as raw function references (`fab.onclick = openXForm`), which caused the browser's click Event object to be silently passed in as the function's `id` argument. This crashed the "New Bilty" flow entirely (blank screen) and caused incorrect default state on other add forms. **Fix:** wrapped every FAB handler in an arrow function (`fab.onclick = () => openXForm()`) across Truck, Driver, Party, Product, and Bilty forms.
2. **Detail screens showing stale/empty data:** Tapping a Truck, Driver, Party, or Bilty in a list (and the Home "Recent Bilties" list) called `go('xDetail')` — which renders immediately — *before* `openXDetail(id)`, which actually loads the correct record. So the detail screen always showed the previous record, one tap behind. **Fix:** reordered so the record loads first, then the screen navigates.

Verified with an automated headless-browser pass (Playwright): create truck → create Bilty with all fields → open Bilty detail → open Truck/Driver/Party detail from their lists → tap a Bilty from the Home screen. No console errors; every detail screen now shows the correct linked data.

## v5 — Multi-Account Support (20 Aug 2026)

Owner asked for the ability to run more than one business inside the app: add one or more accounts, switch between them by tapping the profile/Company Profile option, and each account must be a fully separate entity whose data can never be merged or mixed with another account's. Implemented in `logitrack_prototype.html` and reflected in `LogiTrack_PRD.md` (now v0.6, new Module J).

**Design decision (confirmed with owner before building):** an "account" = a completely separate business — its own Trucks, Drivers, Parties, Products, Bilties, and Company Profile, none of it shared with any other account. Built and reflected in both PRD and prototype in this same round (not deferred).

**Implemented:**
- Only the *active* account's data lives in the app's normal working variables (`trucks`, `drivers`, `parties`, `products`, `bilties`, `nextId`, `companyProfile`) — every existing screen and function keeps working exactly as before, with zero changes needed to 40+ existing functions.
- A new `accounts` registry holds a snapshot of every *inactive* account. `switchAccount(id)` saves the current working data into `accounts[activeAccountId]`, then loads the target account's snapshot into the working variables, resets navigation state, and jumps to Home — so nothing from the old account bleeds into the new one.
- `addNewAccount()` snapshots the current account, creates a brand-new account with completely empty Trucks/Drivers/Parties/Products/Bilties and a blank Company Profile, makes it active, and immediately opens its Company Profile edit form so the owner names the new business right away.
- The **Accounts** section lives inside the Company Profile screen (reached by tapping the company bar under the app name, as requested) — it lists every account with its name, marks the currently active one, lets the owner tap any other to switch, and has a "+ Add New Account" button below the list.
- Seeded a second demo account ("Indore Cargo Movers", with its own separate trucks/drivers/parties/products) alongside the original data (now "Mohit Transport Co.") so the switcher has something real to demonstrate in this prototype.
- **PRD change:** removed the old "No multi-branch/multi-company support — single business entity only for v1" Non-Goal (with a note explaining the reversal) and added Module J documenting the feature, including what's explicitly *not* included yet (no cross-account reporting, no delete-account, no separate logins per account — Admin/Staff roles still apply within whichever account is active).

## v6 — Loading Challan Module (3 Sep 2026)

Owner asked for a new module to log a truck booking the moment it's made — by the owner, an agent, or anyone else booking on the business's behalf — before there's time to prepare a full Bilty. Implemented in `logitrack_prototype.html` and reflected in `LogiTrack_PRD.md` (now v0.7, new Module K).

**Design decisions confirmed with the owner before building:**
- "Freight Challan" (the other document mentioned alongside Bilty) is a **separate document from Bilty**, but its own fields/format will be defined in a future round — this round only reserves its entry point (a "Create Freight Challan" button marked "Coming Soon" on the Challan's Detail view), it does not build it.
- Truck Number and Party Name on the Loading Challan are **linked to the existing Truck Master / Party Master** via the same searchable-autocomplete-with-quick-add pattern already used on the Bilty form — not free text.

**Implemented:**
- New `loadingChallans` data array with fields: Date, Serial Number (auto `LC-####`, editable), Truck (Master-linked), Truck Driver Mobile Number, Truck Owner Mobile Number, Loading Point, Unloading Point, Truck Hire Rate, Truck Booking Rate, Party (Master-linked, auto-fills Party Number from the Party record), Party Number, Weight, and **Notes as a repeatable list** — any number of notes can be added, each kept as its own separate, clearly visible entry rather than one merged text block.

- Reused the Bilty form's quick-add-missing-record pattern for the Truck and Party fields on this form too (new `challanReturnContext`/`quickAddFromChallan`/`returnToChallanWithNew` functions, mirroring the existing `biltyReturnContext` mechanism) — if a truck or party doesn't exist yet, it can be added right there and the Challan resumes with it linked, including correct restoration if the user backs out without saving.

That view has two actions: **"Create Bilty from this Challan"** (fully working — opens a new Bilty pre-filled with the Challan's Party as Consignor, Truck, Weight into the first Goods row, Booking Rate as Freight, and a new **Remarks** field on the Bilty carrying the Loading Point, Unloading Point, and all Notes forward) and **"Create Freight Challan"** (present but intentionally non-functional — "Coming Soon" — since that document isn't specified yet).
- Once a Bilty is created from a Challan, the Challan's Detail view shows a "Bilty Created" link back to that Bilty (tracked via a new `linkedBiltyId` field and `convertingFromChallanId` transient state).
- Added a **Remarks** field to the Bilty form/save/detail (was drafted in the PRD long ago but never built) — general-purpose, not exclusive to Challan conversion, and shown on the Bilty's Detail View/PDF when present.
- Entry points: a new "New Loading Challan" quick action on Home, plus "Loading Challans" added to the "⋮" menu on both Home and the Bilty List (no new bottom-nav icon — kept the nav bar at 5 items, consistent with how Product Master is already surfaced without its own tab).
- **Multi-account isolation (Round 5) extended to this module:** `loadingChallans` and its `nextId.challan` counter are included in every account's snapshot, `switchAccount()`, and `addNewAccount()` — a new account starts with zero Loading Challans, and switching accounts correctly swaps this data too, same as every other module.

**Second bug found & fixed (small, pre-existing, app-wide):** the toast notification (`.toast`) had no `pointer-events:none`, so even after fading out it could still silently swallow taps on whatever button happened to sit at that same screen position (bottom-center, just above the nav bar) — confirmed via Playwright, where a "Save Bilty" button click was being intercepted by an invisible toast still sitting over it. **Fixed** by adding `pointer-events:none` to `.toast` — it's now purely visual and never blocks interaction underneath it, in this new flow and everywhere else in the app.

## v10 — Freight Challan Calculation Engine (5 Sep 2026)

Owner gave six detailed worked examples (in Hinglish) of how a truck's account should actually settle — Hire Rate vs. Booking Rate, Commission, Other Expenses, Munshiyana, advances paid in cash/diesel by either the transport company or the party directly, shortage/TDS deductions, and the reversed "To-Pay" case where the party pays the truck directly at destination. The owner's new examples compute Hire Amount as **Rate × Weight** (Exp1: 1000 × 30mt − 1000 − 300 = 28,700 — matches exactly). **Calculation engine implemented (all six examples verified exactly via Playwright — every number below is a literal check against the owner's own math):**

1. **Normal case (Payment Type = Paid / To-Be-Billed):** Net Payable to Truck = (Hire Rate × Weight) − Shortage Penalty − TDS Amount − sum of Deductions. ## v14 — Freight Challan Calculation Engine Rebuilt from Training Spreadsheet (11 Sep 2026)

**Read and reverse-engineered the spreadsheet's actual formulas** (not just its numbers) using openpyxl, confirming the exact mechanics before writing any app code:
- `SHORTAGE AMOUNT` = (Shortage Weight × Hire Rate) + max(0, Shortage − Tolerance) × Shortage Rate Cutting — and Shortage Rate Cutting is **₹ per MT**, not ₹ per kg as the app had modeled it since Round 10 (values like 2,000 and 15,000 only make sense per-MT; the old ₹/kg-with-×1000-conversion model was a genuine unit mismatch, now corrected).

- `NET PAYABLE TO DRIVER` = Truck Gross − Booking Freight.

## v23w — "Show Booking Rate?" Now Shows the Real Billed Rate, Not the Internal One, When the Bilty Rate Override Is Active (13 Oct 2026)
