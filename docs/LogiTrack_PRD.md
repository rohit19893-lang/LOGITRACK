**Status:** v0.24.0 — Round 24: **a documentation-only round — no application changes.** A complete handoff package was produced so that a different Claude account, with no access to this project's history, can rebuild or integrate LogiTrack end to end without further instruction, ahead of the owner's planned integration into a separate parent application. Three new documents: **`LogiTrack_BRD.md`** (business context, a 40-term domain glossary, the end-to-end lifecycle with both paths, **nine worked numeric scenarios**, and **53 numbered business rules**), **`LogiTrack_Functional_Spec.md`** (technology-agnostic build spec: full data model, all 39 screens, every field and validation, **the complete calculation engine as annotated pseudocode**, output formats, design tokens, 13 non-functional requirements, acceptance criteria, and a clearly separated recommendations section), and **`LogiTrack_Handoff_Brief.md`** (a covering note to paste into the receiving account). See the Build Log's v24 entry for full detail.

## 1. Objective

A mobile application for document and operational management in the logistics and transport industry — covering truck, driver, party (consignor/consignee), product, bilty/GR, and payment management. Built for simplicity, efficiency, and easy sharing of documents. The app is used exclusively by the admin/owner and 1–2 office staff — **not** by drivers.

## 2. Goals

1. Digitize truck, driver, and party master records (replacing paper files/registers), with document upload and instant retrieval.
2. Generate a professional Bilty/GR digitally that can be exported as PDF and shared instantly via WhatsApp/email — replacing the handwritten bilty book.
3. Track freight payments and outstanding dues per party, reducing manual ledger errors and follow-up delays.
4. Give the owner/staff quick reports (trip register, outstanding, revenue) without manual Excel work.
5. Keep the app simple enough for 1–2 non-technical office staff to use daily without training.

## 3. Non-Goals (v1)

*(Note: an earlier draft of this section listed "No multi-branch/multi-company support — single business entity only for v1" as a Non-Goal. The owner reversed this in Round 5 — multi-account support is now in scope for v1. See Module J below.)*

- **No driver-facing app or login.** Drivers continue to communicate via phone/WhatsApp; staff enter data on their behalf. *(Explicit decision — keeps the app single-sided and simple.)*
- **No GPS/live location tracking.** Confirmed out of scope — status updates are manual, not real-time. *(Explicit decision.)*
- **No online payment collection/gateway.** Payments are recorded after being received (cash/UPI/bank transfer), not collected in-app. *(Adds compliance/PCI complexity not needed for internal ops.)*
- **No GST/tax fields on Bilty.** Business does not currently charge GST and has no GSTIN. *(Confirmed by owner — revisit if the business registers for GST later.)*
- **No Expense & Fuel Management, no detailed Access Control/roles in v1.** Both confirmed as real needs but explicitly deferred to Phase 2. *(Owner decision — keeps Phase 1 tightly scoped to master data + Bilty creation.)*
- **No customer-facing portal.** Consignors/consignees do not get login access. *(Separate initiative, not needed for internal efficiency goal.)*

## 4. Common Functionality Across All Modules

- Upload documents from gallery or live camera capture — **multiple photos/documents per record where noted** (Truck, Driver), not just one
- Read-only Detail View with document thumbnails / zoom
- List View with search, filter, and sort
- Export individual records as PDF
- **Selective share via WhatsApp** — on Truck, Driver, and Party detail screens, a "Share via WhatsApp" button opens a checklist of that record's details; only the checked items are included in what's shared. This replaces a blanket "share everything" action.
- Active/Inactive status toggle on master records

**Search UX — applies app-wide wherever a field references another master record** (Consignor, Consignee, Third Party, Truck, Driver, Product): these are **searchable autocomplete fields**, not long static dropdowns. The user types a few letters and matching records appear as suggestions to tap — no scrolling through a full list.

**Quick-add for missing records (added Round 3):** if the party/truck/driver/product being typed doesn't exist yet, the suggestion list includes an "➕ Add '[typed text]' as new [Party/Truck/Driver/Product]" option. Tapping it opens that master's Add form with the typed text pre-filled as the name/number, so the user doesn't retype it. Saving there returns straight back to the in-progress Bilty — nothing else on the Bilty is lost — with the newly created record already selected in the field it was added from. Backing out without saving also correctly restores the in-progress Bilty instead of losing it.

**Contextual "⋮" (more options) menu:** each screen's overflow menu shows only the actions relevant to that screen (e.g., "Export Truck List" on the Truck list, "Share Driver Details" on a Driver's detail page) instead of repeating the same generic links everywhere.

**App header & company branding (added Round 4):** the app name ("LogiTrack") stays at the top of every screen; directly below it, a persistent bar shows the owner's Transport Company name and logo, on every screen, not just Home. Tapping this bar opens the Company Profile (Module I) to view or edit it. This keeps the business's own identity visible throughout the app, not just the app's own name.

**Numeric input fields never change value on an accidental mouse-wheel scroll (added Round 22):** app-wide, a focused numeric field (Freight Amount, Hire/Booking Rate, any Charge/Advance amount, etc.) no longer silently increments or decrements when the page is scrolled with the field still focused — a single global fix, not a per-field one, since this could otherwise corrupt any amount anywhere in the app.

## 5. Platform & Technical Requirements

- **Platform:** Both Android and iOS to be supported.
- **Offline use:** Confirmed as important for practicality (staff may be at depots/routes with poor signal). App must support creating and editing records (Bilty, masters) offline, with data syncing automatically once internet is back. This is a Phase 1 architectural requirement — it affects the tech stack/database choice, so it should be decided before development starts, not bolted on later.
- **Access Control / Roles:** Confirmed needed — an Admin portal (full access) and a Staff portal (limited access) are both required. Detailed permission matrix (what staff can/cannot do — e.g., create Bilty vs. edit payments vs. delete records) will be defined in Phase 2, but the app's login/user structure should be built to support multiple roles from the start so this isn't a rework later.

### L. Freight Challan Module ✅ *Rebuilt from scratch Round 16, after being rolled back Round 15*

Maintains the truck's account (hisab) with the driver/owner for a specific booking — i.e. what the business owes the truck, or needs to recover from it, after Commission, Margin, Other Expenses, Munshiyana, Halting, Kanta Charges, Dala, and Shortage are accounted for. This is a separate ledger from the Bilty, which is the document handed to the party/driver — the Freight Challan is purely an internal money-tracking record between the business and the truck.

Rounds 8–14 built an earlier version of this module (including a calculation engine matched exactly against the owner's own training spreadsheet); the owner asked to delete it entirely in Round 15 and redefine its details fresh. Round 16 is that redefinition, based on the owner's own framing of how the truck-side hisab should work: **entry paths, Payment Type, and Who Pays the Freight all stay exactly as they were** (Rounds 8–13); what's new is the calculation model and a POD-gated settlement flow.

**Two entry paths, one Freight Challan per Loading Challan (unchanged from Round 8–9):**
1. **Direct (no Bilty/LR needed):** from a Loading Challan's Detail view, tap "Create Freight Challan" straight away.
2. **Via Bilty (when a Bilty/LR is needed):** the Bilty is created first (from the Loading Challan, per Module K), and the Freight Challan is then started from that **Bilty's Detail view**.

Both paths converge on the same underlying record — a Loading Challan can only ever have one Freight Challan. Once created via either path, every other entry point shows a **"View Freight Challan"** button instead of offering to create a new one.

**Two decisions pinned down before the truck account can be calculated (unchanged from Round 12):** Payment Type and **Who Pays the Freight?** (Consignor/Consignee/Third Party/Transporter/Broker/Other — roles expanded Round 22, replacing what used to be a specific-party-name search; see Module E for the full rationale, which applies identically here).
- **Via Bilty (Path 2):** both are already decided on the Bilty and carried over automatically.
- **Direct (Path 1, no Bilty):** a short **"Start Freight Challan"** screen asks for both before the record is created.

**Fields carried over automatically from the Loading Challan (and linked Bilty, if any):** Truck, Driver/Owner Mobile, **Owner Name (Round 21)**, Hire Rate, Booking Rate, Load Weight, Payment Type, Who Pays the Freight, and Supplier (Round 20 for the auto-fetch, Round 21 for it becoming a Party Master link). **Consignor/Consignee** are also carried automatically when a Bilty is linked (Path 2); for Path 1 (no Bilty) they're captured on the "Edit Settlement Details" screen *(added Round 19)* as **Party-Master-linked searchable fields** *(changed Round 21 — previously plain free-text fields; searches the full Party list with no Type filter since Round 23n, previously narrowed to Type = Consignor/Consignee respectively)*, defaulting from the Loading Challan's Party field *(Round 20)*, alongside every other new field — the "Start Freight Challan" screen itself stays exactly as it was in Round 17 (Payment Type + Who Pays the Freight only), per the owner's explicit instruction not to spread data entry across more windows than necessary.

**Load Weight and Truck Hire Rate are now visible, editable fields (added Round 20)**, not just silent background values:
- **Load Weight (MT)** — defaults from the **linked Bilty's actual Goods Weight** when one exists (Path 2 — since that's now the final weight, per Module E), or from the **Loading Challan's booking-time weight** with no Bilty (Path 1 — where this field is now the place to correct it to the real final figure, since the Loading Challan's own Weight was never meant to be final). Directly editable either way.
- **Truck Hire Rate (+ Fixed/Per-MT type)** — auto-fetched from the Loading Challan, editable here if it needs correcting before the settlement is finalized.

**Supplier / Truck Owner Details and Broker (added Round 19, on the "Edit Settlement Details" screen):**
- **Supplier / Owner Name** — linked to **Party Master** *(redesigned Round 21 — previously its own silently-remembered `suppliers` list; the owner's Round 21 request to unify Consignor/Consignee/Supplier/Broker under one Party module absorbed this too)*, with the same searchable-autocomplete-and-quick-add pattern as Broker/Consignor/Consignee, showing Mobile/PAN/Address as a read-only reference line once picked. **Searches the full Party list with no Type filter since Round 23n** (previously narrowed to Type = Supplier). **Auto-fetched from the Loading Challan when it was entered there** (Round 20 — Supplier capture on the Loading Challan itself, see Module K), still fully correctable here. **Deliberately not linked to Truck Master** — per the owner's explicit instruction, the same truck can arrive under a different supplier/market operator on a different trip, so cross-referencing "which supplier brought which truck when" is left to a future report rather than a live data relationship.
- **Broker (optional)** — linked to Party Master, per the owner's explicit instruction ("Party Master se hi link karein"). **Gained quick-add capability in Round 21** — previously this field could only search existing Party Master records with no way to create a new Broker from here; it now works exactly like Consignor/Consignee/Supplier. **Searches the full Party list with no Type filter since Round 23n** (previously narrowed to Type = Broker). **Now auto-fetched at creation time (Round 23r)** — from the linked Bilty's own Third Party field when one exists (priority, being the more specific per-Bilty source), else from the originating Loading Challan's own Broker field (Round 23q) when there's no Bilty or its Third Party was left blank. Still fully editable here afterward either way — this only sets the starting value.

**Bilty Expenses shown for reference, when this Freight Challan is linked to a Bilty that has any (added Round 22.2)** — a "Bilty Expenses (from LT-####)" card, listing each of the linked Bilty's own Expense entries (Module E) plus a Total, appears on the Freight Challan Detail so the person settling the truck's account can see what was already charged to the driver on the Bilty without switching screens. **Read-only and reference-only** — same as the Bilty's own display of them — entirely separate from this module's own Charges list below, which is what actually feeds the calculation.

**Settlement Details (on a dedicated "Edit Settlement Details" screen, once the load has actually moved):**
- **Unload Weight** (optional), **Shortage Tolerance (MT)**, **Shortage Rate Cutting (₹/MT)** — Shortage = Load − Unload (using the Load Weight above); Shortage Amount = (Shortage × Hire Rate) + max(0, Shortage − Tolerance) × Shortage Rate Cutting. Doesn't apply when Hire Rate is Fixed.
- **Charges — a dynamic list, driven by the Charge Type Master (Module O)** *(replaced Round 17 — previously six fixed fields: Commission, Other Expenses, Munshiyana, Halting, Kanta Charges, Dala)*. Each charge instance has a Type (picked from the Charge Type Master), a Direction (**Payable** = added to what we owe the truck/driver; **Recoverable** = deducted/recovered from them), an Amount (always entered as a positive ₹ magnitude — Direction alone decides the sign), and an **optional Note (added Round 23k)** — free text for a one-off explanation (e.g. "paid at Bhopal yard," which trip it covers), shown under the charge in this list wherever it's set. **Commission and Halting Charges have a fixed, locked Direction** (Commission is always Recoverable, Halting Charges is always Payable — matching the old hardcoded behaviour exactly), matching real business rules that never change; every other charge type's Direction is editable per instance, so the owner can bend a charge either way for a new example without needing an app change. **Commission can additionally be entered as Fixed (a direct ₹ amount) or Percentage** *(added Round 19)* — when Percentage, the owner enters a % and the amount is computed live off Truck Freight, with both the % and computed ₹ amount stored and shown wherever the charge appears. **"Shortage" added as a Charge Type (added Round 23d), also with a locked Recoverable Direction** — lets a shortage deduction be logged manually through this same Add Charge flow, as a complement to the automatic Weight-based Shortage Amount below (for a one-off case the weight formula doesn't capture); a field-hint on the Charges section spells out the distinction so the two aren't confused for each other. The Charge Type Master itself has **no dedicated screen** — it only surfaces as the Type picker inside this Add Charge form, per the owner's explicit instruction that it stay backend-only.
- **TDS Amount** *(re-added Round 19, retired in Round 14 pending an example)* — a standalone field, always deducted from the truck/driver side of the ledger the same way a Recoverable charge would be, in both calculation branches below. **Can now be a flat ₹ Amount or a Percentage of Truck Freight (added Round 23d)** — a TDS Type toggle (Fixed/Percentage) controls which; in Percentage mode, the owner enters a % and a live hint shows the computed ₹ amount against the current Truck Freight, recalculating automatically if Hire Rate or Weight change later (unlike Commission's Percentage mode, which freezes the amount at the moment the charge is added — TDS stays live since it's a single always-present field, not a list of discrete entries).
- **Diesel Given to Driver (optional)** *(added Round 19)* — Diesel Amount (₹) and Pump Name, shown only when Payment Type is To-Pay, tracked separately from the Advances list below but affecting the books the same way (feeds only the Truck Freight Final reference figure).
- **Advances — a dynamic list** *(changed Round 18, previously three fixed fields: Advance Cash / Advance QR-UPI-NEFT / Advance Diesel; Mode list expanded and Date/Paid To added Round 19)*. **Now shown for every Payment Type (Round 23m) — previously shown only when Payment Type is To-Pay**, which meant a Paid/To-Be-Billed Freight Challan had no way to see or add an Advance at all even though a linked Bilty's own Advance was already silently syncing into it in the background. Each entry records Amount, Mode (**now the app-wide shared list, Round 23v: Cash/NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/Cheque/UPI/Other**, replacing the earlier Cash/Cheque/UPI/NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/Phone Pay/Bank Transfer list — Diesel dropped from this list in Round 19 now that it has its own dedicated field above), **Date**, **Paid To (Driver/Owner)**, an optional **Given By** — the STAFF member who physically handed over the advance, with autocomplete against User Management (Module N) — answering the owner's ask to track "kiske dwara diya gaya hai" (by whom it was given), an optional **Note (added Round 23o)** matching the one already on Add Charge, and a new **Paid By (added Round 23o as "Who Actually Paid," relabeled Round 23v for consistency with the same field everywhere else)** — Self (Company) / Consignor / Consignee / Broker / Other (with a free-text label when Other is picked). "Paid By" is a deliberately separate concept from "Given By": Given By is the staff hand that physically passed the cash over, while Who Actually Paid is which party's money it actually was — the owner asked for this specifically so a Freight Challan with several advances from different sources stays traceable later (e.g. "this much the Consignor gave directly, this much we gave ourselves, this much was arranged via the Broker"). **For To-Pay, the total still feeds only the "Truck Freight Final" reference figure, not the amount recovered from the driver — confirmed as the intended design in Round 22**, after the owner reported an Advance "not coming through" into the calculation; investigation found Advances do persist and display correctly, and the owner confirmed (via a direct decision prompt) this reference-only behavior should stay exactly as it is, matching the original training-spreadsheet examples, and it remains untouched by Round 23m or Round 23o. **For Paid/To-Be-Billed, the total is a real, binding deduction (Round 23m)** — see the calculation branch below. **Since Round 23o, the itemized Advance/Diesel breakdown is no longer shown as a separate section further down the Detail screen** — in both calculation branches it now sits directly beside the figure it explains (right under the single "Advance Given to Driver" summary row for Paid/To-Be-Billed, right under "Truck Freight Final (reference)" for To-Pay), so the settlement reads as one continuous calculation instead of a total followed by an unrelated-looking list underneath it. **Bug fixed (Round 23) — a Bilty's own cash Advance(s) now carry forward automatically** when a Freight Challan is created from that Bilty (Path 2): previously an Advance entered on the Bilty (e.g. ₹10,000 given to the driver at loading) silently dropped out and had to be re-entered by hand on the Freight Challan; - **POD Note, when set, shown as a bold red "⚠ POD Note" banner at the very top of both this screen and the read-only Detail view (added Round 23)** — see Module P — so anything flagged there (a shortage, a delay, anything worth remembering) is impossible to miss while settling the freight.
- **Additional Details** *(added Round 19, Commission Status redesigned and Financial Year removed from the UI Round 23o)* — Balance Payable At (free text, e.g. an office/branch name), Remark (optional), and a Signature Collected Yes/No toggle. **Commission Status is now conditional, not a fixed Pending/Paid dropdown:** if a Commission charge already exists on this Freight Challan's own Charges list (commission already netted out of the truck settlement, Module O/the Charges field above), this prompt is hidden entirely and a hint explains why; otherwise it asks **Was Commission Paid? (Yes/No)** — Yes reveals **Paid Via (Cash/UPI/Other)** and a **Date**, No needs nothing further. **Financial Year is no longer shown or editable anywhere in this UI** (the owner said it wasn't needed) — it's still silently computed from the Freight Challan's date (e.g. "2026-27") and stored on the record in case anything downstream needs it, but never surfaced on the form or the Detail view.

**The two calculation branches, per the owner's Round 16 framing — the arithmetic itself is unchanged since Round 17 aside from TDS now being deducted (Round 19); only the source of Commission/Other Expenses/Munshiyana/Halting/Dala/Kanta Charges changed from six fixed fields to the dynamic Charges list above (summed by Direction):**

1. **Payment Type = To-Pay — we RECOVER from the driver.** The party (per Freight Paid By) pays the driver the full freight directly at delivery, so the business recovers the difference between what the driver actually collected and what they were entitled to:
   - Truck Freight = Load Weight × Hire Rate (or Fixed amount)
   - Booking Freight Expected (what the party is *expected* to pay the driver) = Load Weight × Booking Rate, plus Halting Charges when Freight Paid By is Consignee; otherwise based on the smaller of Load/Unload Weight. **Uses the Bilty Rate instead of the Booking Rate when a rate override is active (added Round 23t)** — see the Bilty's own "Bill the Party at the Booking Rate?" field (Module E) for when a Bilty exists (Path 2); for a Path 1 booking (no Bilty), the identical toggle/field is captured directly on this Freight Challan's own "Edit Settlement Details" screen (`billAtBookingRate`/`billRate`/`billRateType`) instead, since there's no Bilty to carry it from — matching how Consignor/Consignee are already Path-1-only fields on that same screen. Either way, **Truck Freight above is read off the Hire Rate alone and is never affected by this override** — only the party-side recovery figure changes. The Detail view shows a field-hint under Truck Gross naming the actual rate in use whenever this override is active.
   - **Booking Freight (the figure actually used below) — confirmed via POD, not assumed (added Round 23r)**: before POD is marked, this equals the Expected figure above, shown as a running estimate. Once Mark POD's "To-Pay Freight Collection" question (see Module P) is answered — **Yes or No, each with an actual typed amount (changed Round 23x — previously only No asked for an amount; Yes silently assumed the full expected figure)** — that confirmed, actually-typed figure is used here instead, and the Freight Challan Detail shows Expected vs. Received vs. (if there's a shortfall) Balance Still Due from the payer.
   - Truck Gross = Truck Freight + (sum of Payable charges) − (sum of Recoverable charges) − TDS Amount, minus Shortage Amount — except when Freight Paid By is Consignee and there's an actual shortage, where Shortage Amount is deliberately not subtracted (the driver already bears that loss directly with the consignee)
   - **Net to Recover from Driver = Booking Freight − Truck Gross** (shown as "Extra Payable to Driver" in the rare case this comes out negative — which, since Round 23r, is also the natural, correctly-tracked outcome when the party under-paid the driver: the confirmed shortfall now surfaces here as extra owed to the driver rather than being silently absorbed into an inflated recovery figure)
   - **Tracked properly now, instead of showing "Fully Settled" with nothing to log (bug fixed Round 23s)** — previously, whenever this "Extra Payable" case was in play, the `isSettled` check only ever looked at the (always-zero, in this case) recovery figures, so the ledger section showed "✓ Fully Settled" the moment the Freight Challan was created, with no way to log or track any payment made toward the real amount owed — exactly what the owner's screenshot showed: a real ₹50,000 part-payment (recorded as an Advance, which only ever affects the reference-only Truck Freight Final line) wasn't being netted against it at all. This case now tracks its own running total and balance (**"Paid to Driver So Far"** / **"Balance Still Payable to Driver"**, red while > 0), fed by a new **`ledgerDirection`** concept computed from the actual money-flow direction for this specific Freight Challan (not just its static Payment Type) — when Extra Payable applies, entries route through the same **Payment Entries** ledger the Paid/To-Be-Billed branch below uses (money the business pays the driver), never the Recovery Entries ledger (money coming *from* the driver, which doesn't apply here). This `ledgerDirection` logic is shared by every screen that touches this ledger — the Detail view, WhatsApp Share, and Mark Fully Settled — so all three always agree on the same balance and the same array.
   - **"Paid By" (added Round 23s as "Who Actually Paid This")** — a field on both the inline Add Payment/Recovery Entry form and the Mark Fully Settled confirmation form, reusing the existing Advance "source" list (Self (Company) / Consignor / Consignee / Third Party / Broker / Other). **Broadened Round 23v to always show, on every settlement entry regardless of case** — previously gated to only appear for this Extra-Payable case; the owner asked it be asked the same way on every settlement entry that Advances already ask it, so `saveFreightRecovery()` (which previously didn't capture it at all) and the ordinary Recovery Entries ledger now also record it. Every payment/recovery logged now records whether it was the business's own money or a (late/partial) payment the party actually made — directly answering the owner's related ask that this stay traceable once a party ledger exists (see the note at the end of this Fields list below). The pre-existing **`toPayBalanceDue`** ("Balance Still Due from [payer]," Round 23r) is a separate, deliberately untouched figure — what the party still owes overall — while this tracks what the business itself still owes the driver; the owner confirmed both are correct to keep side by side.
   - In the ordinary (non-Extra-Payable) case, tracked in a **Recovery Entries** ledger (Date, Amount, Mode, **Reference/Transaction No. — added Round 17**, Note, **Paid By — broadened here Round 23v**); Truck Freight Final (subtracting the full Shortage Amount, TDS Amount, the Advances total, and Diesel Amount) shown as a secondary reference line. **Settlement consolidated (Round 23v):** the Truck Account card now shows **"Recovered So Far"** / **"Balance to Recover"** directly under the "Net to Recover from Driver" total (mirroring how the Extra-Payable case already showed its own "Paid to Driver So Far"/"Balance Still Payable to Driver" since Round 23s) — the matching balance line that used to sit separately below the ledger entries list further down the page is removed, so the real, current balance is shown in exactly one place.

2. **Payment Type = Paid or To-Be-Billed — we PAY the driver ourselves**, unified into one branch per the owner's instruction: *"is condition me party ka hisab alag se kisi module me karenge, or is module me ye dekhenge ki truck wale ko kya bhada dena hai"* (the party's hisab will be handled in a separate module; this module only looks at what freight we owe the truck). **The Party Reconciliation card and Commission-Recoverable-from-Party flag from the earlier design are removed** — party-side billing is explicitly out of scope for this module now, reserved for a future module:
   - Net Payable to Truck (Gross) = Truck Freight + (sum of Payable charges) − (sum of Recoverable charges) − Shortage Amount − TDS Amount
   - **Final Payable to Truck (added Round 23m) = Net Payable to Truck − Advances Total (the Advances list plus Diesel Amount, same total the To-Pay branch computes)** — previously this branch ignored Advances entirely, so a driver already given cash/UPI against the trip still showed the full freight as payable; the owner asked for this specifically so an Advance recorded either directly here or on the linked Bilty is accounted for and "hamse error bhi kam se kam ho" (so fewer errors happen on our side). A hint appears if the Advance total exceeds the Gross figure (driver overpaid).
   - Tracked in a **Payment Entries** ledger (Date, Amount, Mode, **Reference/Transaction No. — added Round 17**, Note, **Paid By — added Round 23v**) — **Balance = Final Payable to Truck − Payments (changed Round 23m, previously Net Payable − Payments)**, Settled once ≤ 0. **Settlement consolidated (Round 23v):** the Truck Account card now shows **"Paid to Truck So Far"** / **"Balance Payable"** directly under "Final Payable to Truck" — the same balance figure that used to only appear separately below the ledger entries list, now shown in exactly one place.

**POD Received gate — now routes through the dedicated POD Module (Module P, replaced Round 22; previously its own inline multi-photo/PDF upload popup added Round 17):** mirrors the Bilty's own Trip Completed (POD) flag — a **"Mark POD Received"** button on the Freight Challan Detail view opens the shared POD Module's form (see Module P) instead of a local popup, so a POD already recorded from the linked Bilty shows up here too rather than asking twice. ### P. POD (Proof of Delivery) Module ✅ *Added Round 22, at owner's request — a dedicated List+Detail module, over an inline-only alternative, per the owner's own choice*

A single shared record of proof-of-delivery for a trip, reachable from — and shared by — both the Bilty (Module E) and the Freight Challan (Module L), so POD is recorded exactly once per delivery no matter which screen it was started from. Before this round, each of those two modules had its own separate, disconnected POD-marking mechanism (a plain toggle on the Bilty, an upload popup on the Freight Challan); the owner asked for these to be unified into one real module instead, since a POD confirmed on one side should be visible on the other without a second, duplicate entry.

**Fields:**
- Linked Bilty (if raised from/linked to one) and/or linked Freight Challan (if raised from/linked to one) — a record can carry either or both links, resolved automatically: opening POD from a Bilty finds its already-linked Freight Challan (via the Loading Challan → Bilty → Freight Challan chain) and vice versa, so the same record is found and edited from whichever side it's opened from, never duplicated.
- POD Photos — multiple, required (at least one to confirm); simulated uploads in this prototype, same chip/remove pattern as every other document upload in the app.
- Received Weight / Quantity (free text, e.g. "17.8 MT" or "295 Bags") — pre-filled from the linked Freight Challan's Load Weight or the linked Bilty's total Goods Weight, editable.
- Any Damage? — Yes/No, with a Damage Details text area shown only when Yes.
- Status — Received / Not Received, with a recorded date/time each time it's set.
- **POD Date (added Round 23p)** — required date field, defaulting to today, capturing the date the POD is actually being deposited/submitted (which may be a few days after it was physically received, if it's being logged in a bit late) — distinct from the pre-existing auto-generated in-app timestamp (below), which the owner cannot edit or backdate. Shown on the POD Form (top, above the photo upload), the POD Detail view ("POD Date"), and the POD List's subtitle line (replacing what that line used to show). An older POD record saved before this field existed falls back to displaying today's date if opened for edit.
- Logged in App *(the same auto-generated date/time this module has always recorded the instant "Confirm POD Received" is tapped — relabeled "Logged in App" on POD Detail, alongside the new POD Date above, so the two aren't confused with each other)*.
- **Note (added Round 23)** — free text, optional. Shown in a flagged alert card at the top of the Freight Challan's Settlement and Detail screens, so anything noted here (a shortage, a delay, anything the owner wants kept in mind) is impossible to miss while making/settling the freight.
- **Damage details also now flow onto the Freight Challan (added Round 23d)** — previously only the Note (above) synced across; the Damage Yes/No + Damage Details already captured here (see the main Fields list above) now carry onto the linked Freight Challan's alert card too, alongside the Note, whether the Freight Challan already existed when POD was marked or was created afterwards.
- **To-Pay Freight Collection (added Round 23r, Yes/No both now ask for the amount Round 23x)** — shown only when the linked Bilty/Freight Challan's Payment Type is To-Pay, and required before POD can be confirmed. Displays who's expected to pay (per "Freight Paid By" — Consignee/Consignor/Third Party/etc.) and the expected amount for reference, then asks **"Any payment received by the driver from [payer]?"** (relabeled Round 23x from "Has the driver received this from [payer]?", for a simpler, cleaner question) — a plain **Yes/No** toggle (relabeled Round 23x from "Yes — Received in Full"/"No"), and **either answer now reveals the same "Amount Received (₹)" field** (Round 23x — previously only "No" revealed an amount field; "Yes" silently saved the full theoretical expected figure with no way to see or adjust it). Yes prefills the field with the full expected amount, No prefills it with 0, but both are freely editable, and **whatever is actually typed is what's saved and fed into the Freight Challan's own recovery calculation** — not an assumption tied to which button was picked. Saved on the POD record (`toPayFreightReceived`, `toPayAmountReceived`) and synced onto the linked Freight Challan the same way Note/Damage/Received Weight already are, including the same retroactive carry-forward when POD is marked before the Freight Challan even exists (see Module L's Broker/Advances carry-forward pattern). The POD Detail view's own summary line is now a single, accurate "To-Pay Freight: ₹X received" regardless of which option was picked (previously read "Received in Full" vs. "Partial/Not Received — ₹X received so far," which no longer fit now that Yes can carry any typed amount).

It's now a single, understated card shared by both the Note and Damage flags: a thin red accent bar on the left, a light red-tinted background, a small uppercase "⚠ POD Attention" label, and the actual text in normal weight below it (only the "Damage reported:" / "Note:" field labels are bold) — matching the visual weight of every other card/hint in the app rather than standing out as a special, jarring block.

**UI Design:** Reachable via "⋮" → "POD Records" from Home, Bilty List, and Freight Challan List. **List View redesigned Round 23** to show 🚛 Truck Number (bold) as the title, with Loading Date and POD Received Date as the subtitle lines, and a Received/Not Received status badge — replacing the earlier Bilty No./Freight Challan Serial No. + damage-flag display, since the truck and the two dates are what the owner actually scans for here. The Bilty Detail and Freight Challan Detail screens each show either "Mark Trip Completed (POD Received)" / "Mark POD Received" (no record yet) or "View/Edit POD" (a record already exists) — never both, and never a way to create a second record for the same delivery.

**Validation:** At least one POD photo is required to confirm a new POD or re-confirm one marked Not Received.

**Flow:**
1. From the Bilty Detail or Freight Challan Detail, tap the Mark POD button → fill in Photos, Received Weight/Qty, Damage → Confirm → lands on the POD Detail view, with both linked documents now reflecting the update.
